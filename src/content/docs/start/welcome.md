JumpTracker Pro is a mobile-first app for professional skydivers. It replaces spreadsheets and paper logs with one place to record your work, track what you're owed, and send invoices.

This site goes deeper than the app itself. It explains the parts that are easy to get wrong, and it shows how other jumpers set the app up for their work.

## What the app does

- **Log your work.** Record a whole day at once with **Log Day**, or a single entry with **Log Jump**. Track the location, quantities, optional start and end times, and tips.
- **Work at more than one location.** Each dropzone has its own categories, services and rates.
- **Track what you earn and what you're owed.** Mark entries as paid or pending, and see today's, this week's and this month's earnings on your dashboard.
- **Send invoices.** Generate an invoice for a date range and location, export it as a PDF, and track whether it has been paid.
- **Bring your history with you.** Import a Burble transaction history instead of typing it in again.

## Where to start

- **New to the app?** Set up your locations and services in **Settings**, then log your first day with **Log Day**. The in-app tour can be replayed if you want a walkthrough.
- **Does your dropzone use Burble?** Follow [Import Your Burble Logbook](/technical/import-burble-logbook/) to bring your history over and set up a dropzone with the import wizard instead of manually.
- **Confused by a number on your dashboard or invoice?** See [How Jump Counts and Totals Are Calculated](/technical/how-totals-are-calculated/).
