---
title: How Jump Counts and Totals Are Calculated
description: Why your jump count, service counts and earnings can differ, and what each number includes.
---

JumpTracker Pro shows counts and earnings on the dashboard, in the Summary page and on invoices. They come from a few simple rules. Once you know them, the numbers make sense.

## Service types decide what counts as a jump

Every service you set up has a **service type**:

| Service type | What it is | Counts as a jump? |
|---|---|---|
| **Jump** | A core skydiving service, such as a tandem or an AFF jump | Yes |
| **Jump modifier** | An add-on to a jump, such as a video or photo package | No |
| **Pack job** | Packing a parachute | No, it is counted separately as pack jobs |
| **Other** | Anything else you want to track | No |

Jump modifiers exist so that a tandem with a handcam video is **one jump**, not two. The add-on still earns money, but it does not add to your jump count.

## What each number includes

- **Jump count:** only services of type *Jump*.
- **Service counts:** every service you logged, whatever its type.
- **Earnings:** the payment amount of every service, whatever its type. A video add-on raises your earnings but not your jump count.
- **Tips:** not included in category or service totals. Tips are recorded per work day, not per jump.

## Why tips and hours show a dash on some tabs

On the Summary page, tips and hours appear only on the **All Services** tab. **Paid Services** and **Pending Services** show a dash for them. Tips and hours belong to a work session (one per day), not to an individual service, so they have no paid or pending status.

## How invoice totals are grouped

Invoices group your work by **category** first (for example Tandem), then by service inside it. Each category also shows a per-location breakdown. Categories and services appear in the order you set in your settings.

Category totals and location totals always add up to the same overall figure.
