Sometimes a dropzone changes how it pays, or you decide your categories and services should be organized differently. Rather than editing everything in place, you can archive the old setup and start a clean one. Your past logs stay exactly as they were, and new work is logged against the new setup.

Categories and services belong to a location, so the approach is to archive the old location and add a new one for the same dropzone.

## 1. Archive the old location

1. Open **Settings** and find your dropzone under **Locations**. If it isn't the default location shown, tap **Show all (n)**.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Add a suffix to the **Location Name**, such as `Skydive Example - Archive 2024-2026`, then save.

Everything logged there stays on it, and your old invoices and Logbook entries are unchanged. Only the name is different.

## 2. Add the new location

1. Under **Locations**, tap **Add**.
2. Enter the dropzone's original name, along with any billing details you use for invoices.
3. Save it.

The new location starts with the standard starter categories and services (Tandem, Outside Video, AFF and Coach).

## 3. Set up categories and services

Rename, delete or add categories and services until they match the new structure. See [Categories and Services](/start/categories-and-services/) for how to name them and choose the right **Service Type**.

If you want the new location to be the one preselected when you log, edit it and turn on **Set as default location**.

## 4. Log to the new location

From here on, choose the new location when you log with **Log Day** or **Log Single**. To look at either setup on its own, filter the Logbook by location. Your archived work still counts toward your overall totals for the dates it was logged.

:::tip
When you add a service with **Add to all locations**, it is also added to your archived location. Leave that option off if you want the archive to stay as it was.
:::
